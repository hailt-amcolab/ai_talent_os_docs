## What the system does

Talents (start ~30, +10/month, ~100 soon; target 200–300, later 1,000; includes approved non-affiliated applicants) post daily to Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube (same media + caption to all three) with help from a per-talent **AIコーチ**. The goal is 10k followers per platform (default, editable per talent). Managers (~5) approve sign-ups and set talents up once. After that, the AI does the daily work and managers only handle notifications.

Daily loop: 8:00 AI coach reviews yesterday and proposes 3 theme candidates → talent picks one → AI writes caption, hashtags, shot list (plus a script for video) → talent uploads photo/video → talent adopts the draft (optionally requesting manager approval) → scheduled post to all 3 SNS → metrics are pulled back in for the next day. **Changed by the 確認シート (2026-11-17):** this loop now applies to the 要確認 (confirm-each-post) mode only; in 自動投稿 mode the talent uploads materials once for a period and the system writes and posts without per-post adoption; proposal cadence and send time band (morning/noon/evening) come from the AI instructions ([KS] 確認14, 18, 19, 25).

Two front ends:
- **Talent app**: smartphone PWA (iOS push needs 16.4+). Talents see only their own data and never log in to the admin.
- **Admin (管理画面)**: PC browser first but must also be readable on a phone browser, 2FA login. Every manager sees every talent, including all coach conversations, ; approval requests are routed by assignment/category under No.6 (fallback still open), rather than assuming every Manager receives every request.

## Business rules that shape the design

- Posts have 6 states, and success/failure is tracked **per SNS**. Talent status, video, SNS connection, and billing also get state machines (details in docx §5-1, to be finalized).
- Manager-configurable defaults: morning message 8:00 (now sent by morning/noon/evening time band, [KS] 確認18); reminder 2h before post time; nudge after 3 days without a post; manager alert after 7 (both day counts to be re-confirmed, [KS] 確認19); weekly review on Monday. - Approval is a per-talent setting. The talent decides whether to request it (the AI does not classify job posts).
